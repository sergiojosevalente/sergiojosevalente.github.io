Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

DokuCel is a free PDF toolkit for Windows, made by Sergio Jose Valente Da Cruz ("the author"). This policy explains what happens to your files and information when you use the DokuCel desktop app.

Summary

  • DokuCel does not collect, sell or share personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no telemetry.
  • Your PDFs and other files are processed on your own computer and are never uploaded by the desktop app.
  • DokuCel connects to the internet only to look for updates on GitHub, and you can turn that off.

Information stored on your computer

WhatWhere and how
Settings (default folders, OCR language, update check)%APPDATA%\DokuCel
Saved workflows, saved signatures and interface preferences (language, theme)The app window's own data folder, %APPDATA%\DokuCel.exe
Copies of the files you process and the results, while a task runs%LOCALAPPDATA%\DokuCel; deleted automatically after 6 hours
Files you saveThe folder you choose

The author never receives any of this information. Uninstalling DokuCel offers to delete it.

Network connections

  • Updates: when you choose Help → Check for updates, or at start-up if that check is on (Settings), DokuCel contacts api.github.com and github.com. GitHub receives the usual information of any web request, such as your IP address, under GitHub's privacy statement. Downloaded updates are checked with a digital signature before they are installed.
  • Helper programs: OCR (Tesseract), Office conversion (LibreOffice) and HTML to PDF (Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome) run on your computer. When you convert an HTML file, the browser may load images or styles that the HTML file itself links to.
  • Links you click (for example to this website) open in your web browser.

Open with DokuCel

The installer adds DokuCel to Windows' Open with list for PDF, Office, image, HTML and text files. It does not change your default apps. Uninstalling DokuCel removes these entries.

Web version

DokuCel can also be run as a web application on an organization's own server. In that case your files are uploaded to that server, processed there and deleted automatically after 6 hours, and the organization that runs the server (not the author) is responsible for it, including any sign-in it requires.

Children

DokuCel is a document tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: sergiojosevalente@gmail.com, or open an issue at github.com/sergiojosevalente/dokucel.
